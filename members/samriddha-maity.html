01 / About

Building with purpose.

Samriddha Maity is the founder of CyberDeep Corporate, an informal student-led organization bringing together digital projects and connected teams under a shared direction.

His work spans software development, full-stack engineering and cybersecurity. He is interested in building useful digital products, developing dependable systems and creating a clear structure in which independent projects can grow.

Work across the organization

At CyberDeep Corporate, Samriddha provides organizational direction and helps guide the wider portfolio. The organization’s connected units have distinct responsibilities:

  • SamWorks Studios — software, product development and engineering.
  • Union Foundation — documentation and organizational support.

Approach

His approach is centered on independent project identities, shared standards and long-term maintainability: each venture should have room to do its own work while remaining connected to a thoughtful foundation.
